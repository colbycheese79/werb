Preface to the World English Reader's Bible: The New Testament
The New Testament was not originally written with verses, subject headings, or margin notes. It was delivered as a collection of living testimonies, historical accounts, and pastoral letters meant to be read, heard, and digested as whole works. Over the centuries, the accumulation of modern reference tools—while invaluable for verse-by-verse study—has inadvertently fragmented the text, often turning a flowing narrative or structured argument into a collection of isolated proof-texts.
The World English Reader's Bible (New Testament) is an independent effort to restore the natural, uninterrupted flow of the biblical text. By stripping away the typographic conventions of traditional reference editions, this volume provides a clean, distraction-free canvas designed specifically for immersive, sustained reading.
To cultivate an environment focused entirely on the text itself, this edition removes verse numbers, chapter headings, and footnotes. The text flows continuously in standard paragraph form, allowing you to follow the writer's train of thought without numerical interruptions or uninspired section dividers.
Rather than adhering to the traditional canonical sequence, this edition reorganizes the twenty-seven books of the New Testament into three distinct, logical groupings to highlight historical, thematic, and authorship connections that are easily overlooked in standard layouts.
The first section, The Messiah, opens by pairing the Gospel of Luke directly with its historical sequel, the Acts of the Apostles. Together, they form a continuous, two-part narrative tracking the life, ministry, and resurrection of Jesus Christ, followed immediately by the birth and geographic expansion of the early Church.
The second section, The Letters, brings together the entire Pauline corpus. Reordered to group his operational correspondence and pastoral guidance clearly, this section allows you to engage deeply with Paul’s theology and oversight of the early Christian communities, moving from 1 and 2 Thessalonians through to Titus.
The final section, The Community, compiles the remaining historical testimonies and general epistles, starting with Matthew and Hebrews and concluding with the three letters of John. This arrangement offers a multi-faceted look at Christian life, endurance, and theology through the eyes of the other apostles and early church leaders, culminating in the triumphant apocalyptic vision of Revelation.
The underlying text for this edition is the World English Bible (WEB), a highly accurate, modern English translation that stands as a direct revision of the American Standard Version (ASV) of 1901. Because the translators of the World English Bible generously dedicated their entire work to the public domain, the text is completely free from copyright restrictions. This reader's configuration is likewise offered freely to the global church, fully available for reading, copying, redistribution, and study without barrier.
May the reading of these sacred writings lead to deeper faith in Christ, greater love for His Church, and steadfast hope in the promises of God.
