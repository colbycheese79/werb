Copyright & Permissions

The underlying text of this edition is the World English Bible (WEB), which is a modern English revision of the American Standard Version of 1901.


The translators and publishers of the World English Bible have explicitly dedicated the text to the public domain. It is not subject to copyright restrictions, and permission is not required to copy, quote, publish, or distribute the biblical text itself.


World English Reader's Bible Layout


This specific digital edition, titled the World English Reader's Bible (New Testament), represents an independent editorial configuration. The distinct structural arrangement (including the reorganization into three custom narrative groupings), the removal of traditional chapter and verse typography, and the clean paragraph formatting have been prepared by the editor.


To honor the spirit of the underlying translation, this reader's arrangement and layout design are also dedicated to the public domain under a CC0 1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0) Public Domain Dedication. You are free to copy, modify, distribute, and perform this work, even for commercial purposes, all without asking permission.
