# World English Reader's Bible (WERB) — New Testament

An independent, distraction-free digital reader's edition of the New Testament, re-ordered into structural and narrative groupings for an immersive reading experience.

---

## Overview

The **World English Reader's Bible (New Testament)** is designed for sustained, uninterrupted reading. By removing modern typographic additions and rearranging the books into a flowing, logical order, this edition allows you to engage with the text as a collection of continuous narratives, letters, and testimony.

Like traditional "reader's editions," this project strips away the formatting overhead of reference Bibles to let the text speak for itself.

## Key Features

*   **No Verse Numbers:** Text flows naturally in paragraphs, removing the fragmentation caused by modern verse markers.
*   **No Chapter Headings:** Uninspired section headers and editorial titles are omitted to preserve the original narrative transitions.
*   **No Footnotes:** Textual variants and cross-references are removed from the pages to ensure a clean visual canvas.

---

## Book Order & Structure

Rather than following the traditional canonical order, this edition arranges the New Testament into three distinct groupings based on historical, authorship, and narrative connections. This layout helps highlight the organic relationships between the texts.

### 1. The Messiah (The Luke-Acts Framework)
This section pairs the Gospel of Luke directly with its historical sequel, tracking the life of Christ and the immediate expansion of the early Church.
*   Luke, Acts

### 2. The Letters (The Pauline Corpus)
The epistles of Paul, arranged to group his operational and pastoral correspondence clearly.
*   1 & 2 Thessalonians, 1 & 2 Corinthians, Galatians, Romans, Colossians,Ephesians, Philemon, Philippians, 1 & 2 Timothy, Titus

### 3. The Community (The General Epistles & Other Gospels)
The remaining historical accounts and letters, concluding with the apocalyptic vision of Revelation.
*   Matthew, Hebrews, James, Mark, 1 & 2 Peter, Jude, John, 1, 2, & 3 John, Revelation

## Textual Basis & License

The text used in this project is sourced from the **World English Bible (WEB)**, a modern English translation that is a revision of the American Standard Version (ASV) of 1901. 

The World English Bible is explicitly dedicated to the **public domain**. Because the underlying text is free of copyright restrictions, this reader's configuration is also free to copy, distribute, modify, and share without permission.

## Tracking Updates from the World English Bible
This edition will track updates to the World Enlish Bible and apply any updates to the text that affect the New Testament text.
